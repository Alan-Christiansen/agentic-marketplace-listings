# Facebook Marketplace Guidance
## Use this file when
The listing targets Facebook Marketplace. Load `Seller Profile.md` and `Listing Template.md` from this folder alongside it.
## Seller input
The seller may provide free-form notes, a feature list, dimensions, photos, or any combination of these. Preserve the meaning of the supplied information, distinguish photo evidence from seller statements, and retain useful uncertainties.
## Item identification and preparation
Identify the item as accurately as the available evidence permits, including its model, relevant specifications, condition, completeness, and included accessories. Never invent unsupported details. Ask for missing information when it blocks an accurate or safe recommendation.

Flag cleaning, testing, measurements, better photos, missing accessories, or other minor preparation when it could materially improve value or buyer confidence. Provide practical photo recommendations and useful search terms when they would strengthen the listing.
## Market research and pricing
Research recent, geographically relevant Facebook Marketplace asking prices when useful. Use verified sold prices from eBay or other appropriate markets to strengthen the analysis, especially for rare or specialized items. Clearly distinguish asking prices from verified sold prices and link or identify the supporting sources.

Adjust comparisons for condition, location, demand, seasonality, included accessories, selling fees, shipping, and the difference between asking and realized prices. Explain material uncertainties and give the pricing recommendation a confidence level.

Provide:
- relevant comparable listings and sources;
- an estimated fair-market range;
- a recommended initial listing price;
- a likely selling range;
- a proposed lowest acceptable price for the seller to approve; and
- recommended timing and amounts for price reductions when interest is weak.

Treat every price, negotiation floor, and price-reduction plan as a recommendation until the seller approves it.
## Platform fields
Prepare these fields:
- Title
- Price
- Category
- Condition
- Description
## Drafting guidance
Favor clear everyday language and put the information a local buyer needs near the beginning. Describe what the item is and lead with its supported practical value or buyer experience rather than a wall of specifications. Keep specifications short and scannable without dropping details that materially affect value or fit.

Describe only supported condition details and visible flaws. Avoid inventing urgency, scarcity, backstory, dimensions, included accessories, or availability. Recommend a more suitable sales channel when Facebook Marketplace is unlikely to produce a good outcome.
## Buyer-facing copy
Write `Title`, `Condition`, and `Description` as the seller speaking directly to a potential buyer. State supported item facts naturally; do not narrate the inspection process or evidence provenance. Do not use phrases such as “appears,” “the photos show,” “in the supplied photos,” “I observed,” “visible evidence,” or “you would expect” unless the seller asks for that framing.

Keep uncertainty and evidence provenance in `Photo-Derived Observations` and `Research Notes`, not in buyer-facing fields. Do not turn that boundary into permission to invent a condition claim: use a concise direct condition statement only when seller facts or clear visible evidence supports it; otherwise leave the condition unresolved.

For example, given seller facts for three woven nesting baskets with handles, the description can read:

> Set of three large woven nesting storage baskets with built-in side handles. Great for blankets, toys, linens, pantry storage, or keeping a living room organized. They nest together when not in use.
>
> Sizes:
>
> - Small: 16 in W × 12 in D × 7 in H
> - Medium: 18 in W × 14 in D × 8 in H
> - Large: 22 in W × 18 in D × 8.5 in H
>
> Good used condition with a natural woven finish.
>
> Local pickup only.
## Selling support and safety
When requested, support buyer screening, negotiation, bundling, inventory tracking, pickup planning, and scam avoidance. Protect the seller's personal information and physical safety, flag suspicious behavior, and preserve existing working material and accurate listing state. Draft or recommend actions without posting, contacting buyers, accepting offers, committing to a price, or arranging a meeting unless separately authorized.

Platform fields and policies change. Verify current requirements when a choice depends on them rather than treating this guide as a complete platform specification.
