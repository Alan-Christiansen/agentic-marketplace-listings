# Facebook Marketplace Guidance
## Seller input
Notes, a feature list, dimensions, photos, or any mix. Keep the seller's meaning and any useful uncertainty.
## Identification and preparation
Identify the item as precisely as the evidence allows: model, key specifications, condition, completeness, and included accessories. Flag cleaning, testing, measurements, better photos, or missing parts when they would materially improve price or buyer confidence, and suggest useful search terms.
## Research and pricing
Research recent, local Facebook Marketplace asking prices when useful. Use verified eBay or other sold prices to strengthen the analysis, especially for rare or specialized items. Label asking prices and sold prices separately and cite sources. Adjust for condition, location, demand, season, accessories, fees, shipping, and the gap between asking and realized prices.

Record in `Research Notes`:
- comparables and sources;
- a fair-market range;
- a recommended initial price;
- a likely selling range;
- a proposed lowest acceptable price;
- price-reduction timing and amounts if interest is weak;
- a confidence level and the main uncertainties.
## Platform fields
Title, Price, Category, Condition, Description.
## Drafting
Use clear everyday language and put what a local buyer needs first. Say what the item is and lead with its practical value, not a wall of specifications. Keep specifications short and scannable without dropping details that affect value or fit. Describe only supported condition details and visible flaws. Never invent urgency, scarcity, backstory, dimensions, accessories, or availability.
## Buyer-facing copy
Write `Title`, `Condition`, and `Description` as the seller speaking directly to a potential buyer. State supported item facts naturally; do not narrate the inspection process or evidence provenance. Do not use phrases such as “appears,” “the photos show,” “in the supplied photos,” “I observed,” “visible evidence,” or “you would expect” unless the seller asks for that framing.

Keep uncertainty and evidence provenance in `Photo-Derived Observations` and `Research Notes`, not in buyer-facing fields. Do not turn that boundary into permission to invent a condition claim: use a concise direct condition statement only when seller facts or clear visible evidence supports it; otherwise leave the condition unresolved.

For example, given seller facts for three woven nesting baskets with handles, the description can read:

> Set of three large woven nesting storage baskets with built-in side handles. Great for blankets, toys, linens, pantry storage, or keeping a living room organized. They nest together when not in use.
>
> Sizes:
>
> - Small: 16 in W × 12 in D × 7 in H
> - Medium: 18 in W × 14 in D × 8 in H
> - Large: 22 in W × 18 in D × 8.5 in H
>
> Good used condition with a natural woven finish.
>
> Local pickup only.
## Beyond the listing
When asked, help with buyer screening, negotiation, bundling, pickup planning, and scam avoidance, protecting the seller's personal information and safety. Recommend another channel when Facebook Marketplace is a poor fit. Verify current platform requirements when a choice depends on them.
