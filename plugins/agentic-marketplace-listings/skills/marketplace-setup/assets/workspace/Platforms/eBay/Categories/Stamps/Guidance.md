# eBay Stamp Guidance
## Purpose
Prepare an eBay stamp listing using the exact output fields in this category template. This guide does not grade, authenticate, or catalog a stamp from insufficient evidence.
## Platform fields
Prepare these fields:
- Title
- Price
- Seller Notes
- Place of Origin
- Quality
- Grade
- Country of Origin
- Certification
- Category
- Item Description
## Useful seller-supplied facts
Capture only what is known or directly supported:
- country or issuing region;
- year or issue period;
- denomination;
- subject or issue name;
- catalog system and catalog number;
- mint, used, or other condition description;
- gum, hinge, cancellation, centering, perforation, watermark, and faults when relevant;
- single stamp, set, block, cover, sheet, or other format;
- quantity;
- certification or provenance when documented.
## Preparation and photos
Do not clean, soak, press, regum, rehinge, separate, or otherwise alter a stamp merely to improve its appearance. Flag any handling or preservation question for the seller and recommend appropriate specialist advice when an intervention could affect condition or value.

Recommend clear, color-accurate photos or scans of the front and back, along with useful close views of cancellation, centering, perforations, gum or hinge condition, overprints, plate markings, margins, and visible faults when relevant. Show certificates, expert marks, album pages, mounts, or other included material clearly without treating their presence as proof of authenticity. Make it visually unambiguous whether the listing is for a single stamp, set, block, cover, sheet, lot, or collection.
## Research and pricing
Prioritize recent verified eBay sold results for genuinely comparable stamps. Match as closely as the evidence permits on country or issuing region, issue, denomination, catalog identification, format, mint or used state, condition, centering, gum, hinge status, cancellation, faults, certification, and quantity. Use current asking prices, catalog values, auction archives, and dealer listings as clearly labeled context rather than treating them as realized market value.

Account for material differences between apparently similar stamps and explain when identification or condition uncertainty makes the pricing range broad. Include relevant sources, an estimated fair-market range, a recommended listing format and initial price, a likely selling range, a proposed lowest acceptable price for the seller to approve, and a confidence level with important uncertainties.
## Drafting guidance
Use precise, professional, collector-facing language without implying expertise or certainty beyond the evidence. Put the strongest supported identification in the title, keep specialist terminology consistent across all fields, and describe faults plainly. Avoid hype and do not call a stamp rare, scarce, valuable, investment-grade, or authenticated unless reliable evidence supports the claim.
## Accuracy boundary
Do not invent missing specialist details or convert a casual condition description into a formal grade. Preserve blanks or explicit unknowns for unsupported platform fields. Do not conflate Place of Origin with Country of Origin. Ensure the title, Seller Notes, structured fields, Category, and Item Description do not contradict one another.
