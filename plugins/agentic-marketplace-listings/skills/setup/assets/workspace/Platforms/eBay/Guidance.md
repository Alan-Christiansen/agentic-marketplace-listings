# eBay Guidance
## Use this file when
The listing targets eBay. Load `Seller Profile.md` and the selected listing template alongside it. When a category-specific folder applies, also load its `Guidance.md` and use its `Listing Template.md` instead of the general template.
## General listing output
For categories without a dedicated template, prepare the title, price, category, condition, quantity, relevant item specifics, item description, and seller-supplied shipping or fulfillment details. Confirm exact fields against the current listing form when they materially affect the draft.
## Drafting guidance
Use precise, searchable language without keyword stuffing. Keep structured specifics consistent with the title and description. Never infer an identifier, grade, authenticity, completeness, defect, or shipping promise merely because a field is commonly available.

eBay categories, item specifics, title limits, and policies change. Verify the current listing form for the selected category before encoding or relying on exact requirements.
