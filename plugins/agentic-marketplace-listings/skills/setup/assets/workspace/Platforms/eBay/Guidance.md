# eBay Guidance
## Use this file when
The listing targets eBay. Load `Seller Profile.md` and the selected listing template alongside it. When a category-specific folder applies, also load its `Guidance.md` and use its `Listing Template.md` instead of the general template.
## Seller input
The seller may provide free-form notes, item facts, dimensions, photos, specialist terminology, or any combination of these. Preserve the meaning of supplied information, distinguish photo evidence from seller statements, and retain useful uncertainties.
## Item identification and preparation
Identify the item as accurately as the available evidence permits, including its maker or issuer, model or catalog reference, relevant specifications, condition, completeness, quantity, and included material. Never invent or overstate an identifier, date, variety, grade, authenticity, certification, provenance, defect, or included accessory. Ask for missing information when it blocks an accurate or safe recommendation.

Flag cleaning, testing, measurements, additional research, better photos, or other preparation when it could materially improve value or buyer confidence. Do not recommend cleaning a collectible when doing so could reduce its value; preserve the item's current state until appropriate category guidance or specialist advice supports an action.

Recommend clear photos of the actual item, including multiple useful angles, identifying marks, and close views of condition details or flaws. The primary image should show the complete item clearly against an uncluttered background. Make it visually unambiguous whether the listing is for one item, a set, or a lot.
## Market research and pricing
Prioritize recent verified eBay sold results and appropriate eBay research tools when useful. Use current eBay asking prices and relevant specialist markets as supporting context, clearly distinguishing asking prices from verified sold prices. Prefer genuinely comparable examples and identify or link the sources.

Adjust comparisons for condition, completeness, grade or certification when documented, scarcity, demand, seasonality, quantity, included material, selling fees, shipping, insurance, returns exposure, and the difference between asking and realized prices. Explain material uncertainties and give the pricing recommendation a confidence level.

Provide:
- relevant comparable listings and sources;
- an estimated fair-market range;
- a recommended listing format when the evidence favors fixed price or auction;
- a recommended initial listing price;
- a likely selling range;
- a proposed lowest acceptable price for the seller to approve; and
- recommended timing and amounts for price reductions when interest is weak.

Treat every price, offer threshold, listing-format choice, and price-reduction plan as a recommendation until the seller approves it.
## General listing output
For categories without a dedicated template, prepare the title, price, category, condition, quantity, relevant item specifics, item description, and seller-supplied shipping or fulfillment details. Confirm exact fields against the current listing form when they materially affect the draft.
## Drafting guidance
Use precise, searchable language without keyword stuffing or unsupported promotional claims. Put the strongest identifying terms in the title and complete all relevant item specifics supported by the evidence. Keep the title, category, condition, quantity, structured specifics, photos, and description consistent.

Write a clear, professional description that lets a buyer understand exactly what is included and assess the item's condition. State material flaws plainly and show them in the photos when possible. Include provenance or history only when the seller supplied it or reliable documentation supports it. Never infer an identifier, grade, authenticity, completeness, defect, or shipping promise merely because a field is commonly available.

Recommend a more suitable sales channel when eBay is unlikely to produce a good outcome.
## Selling support and safety
When requested, support buyer-question drafts, offer evaluation, bundling, inventory tracking, packing and shipping planning, and scam avoidance. Protect the seller's personal information, preserve existing working material and accurate listing state, and flag suspicious behavior. Draft or recommend actions without posting, messaging buyers, accepting offers, committing to a price, purchasing shipping, or changing fulfillment terms unless separately authorized.

eBay categories, item specifics, title limits, and policies change. Verify the current listing form for the selected category before encoding or relying on exact requirements.
