# Operating Policy
Applies to every Agentic Marketplace Listings skill. Skills and guidance files point here instead of repeating these rules.
## Sources of truth
- An item folder's parent lifecycle folder under `Listings/` is its status: `1 - Building`, `2 - Ready`, `3 - Posted`, `4 - Sold`, or `5 - Closed`.
- `Listing.md` holds the item facts and listing data.
- `Dashboard.base` is a live derived view. Never regenerate it.
- `Platforms/<Platform>/Seller Profile.md` controls voice and seller preferences. Its approved examples teach style only; never reuse their facts, prices, or condition claims.
- Guidance files and templates describe requirements and structure, not item facts.
## Workspace
A workspace is initialized when it contains `How to Use.md`, `Dashboard.base`, `Platforms/`, and the five lifecycle folders. If it is not, recommend `marketplace-setup` and stop.
## Item identification
Resolve an existing listing from an explicit path, the current `Listing.md`, or a distinctive item name searched across all lifecycle folders. If more than one listing could match, show the candidates and ask. Never write while the target is ambiguous.
## Fact integrity
- Never invent identity, model, measurements, condition, defects, provenance, price, catalog data, grade, authenticity, or platform requirements.
- Keep seller facts, photo evidence, research, and inference distinguishable in the intake and research sections.
- Leave unsupported fields blank or marked unknown. Ask only when a missing fact blocks an accurate next step.
## Records
- Dates are unquoted `YYYY-MM-DD` values in the seller's local date. Prices are numbers. Never treat an omitted date as today.
- Track only the current posted price and date and the sold price and date. No fees, shipping costs, net proceeds, or price history.
- Follow the template's heading layout: no blank line directly before or after a heading.
## Photos
Working photos live beside `Listing.md`. Do not create an originals or archive folder. Do not resize, convert, or replace photos unless the seller asks.
## Authority
The skills organize and draft inside the workspace. Never post externally, contact buyers, accept offers, commit to a price, arrange meetings, purchase shipping, delete seller data, or install anything without separate authorization. Every price, floor, and reduction plan is a recommendation until the seller approves it.
